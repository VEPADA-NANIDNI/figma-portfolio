About

01 — Background

I'm a Computer Science engineering student who found design through building things that needed to actually work for someone else. That's still how I approach every project — start with the person using the screen, not the screen itself.

At StepUpMark, I design the LetsHyre hiring platform end to end: wireframes, component systems, and the navigation logic that connects employers, candidates, and admins. I came in as an intern in October 2025 and moved into a part-time designer role within two months, largely because I was comfortable working with limited briefs and figuring the rest out through research.

Outside design, I host and coordinate events on campus — I led the central hosting committee for RGUKT's annual technical fest and anchor institutional cultural and technical forums. It's a different muscle, but it feeds the same instinct: read a room, communicate clearly, keep things moving.
